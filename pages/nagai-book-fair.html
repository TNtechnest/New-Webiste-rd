Nagai Book Fair

The Nagai Book Fair is a vibrant celebration of reading, knowledge, and community engagement. Designed to inspire young minds and strengthen reading culture in Nagapattinam, the event brings together publishers, authors, educators, and book lovers under one roof. Through interactive sessions, cultural programs, and curated book collections, we aim to build a powerful literary movement.

Our Initiative

The Nagai Book Fair focuses on encouraging reading habits among students and the public. By creating an inclusive platform for publishers, writers, and readers, we foster intellectual growth, creativity, and lifelong learning within the community.
