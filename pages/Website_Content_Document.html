Project: Nagai Book Fair

Category: A Reading Movement | URL: nagai-book-fair.html

Nagai Book Fair 2025

A Reading Movement

Project Overview

The Nagai Book Fair is a vibrant celebration of reading, knowledge, and community engagement. Designed to inspire young minds and strengthen reading culture in Nagapattinam, the event brings together publishers, authors, educators, and book lovers under one roof. Through interactive sessions, cultural programs, and curated book collections, we aim to build a powerful literary movement.

Our Initiative

The Nagai Book Fair focuses on encouraging reading habits among students and the public. By creating an inclusive platform for publishers, writers, and readers, we foster intellectual growth, creativity, and lifelong learning within the community.

Key Achievements

  • Transformed campaign into a state-level reading movement
  • Interactive sessions and cultural programs
  • Community engagement and literacy promotion
  • 50% reach achievement

Project: Art Academy

Poonichierakadal Art Academy

Branding & Design Work

Project Overview

We collaborated with Poonichierakadal Art Academy to redefine their brand identity and digital presence. Our focus was to elevate their positioning, strengthen visual storytelling, and create a strategy that helps increase painting sales while building a strong artistic community.

  • Storytelling-driven campaigns
  • Attracting collectors and art enthusiasts

Strategic Goals

  • Digital presence strengthening
  • Increase in painting sales

Project: Visual RK

Category: Design | URL: event-poster.html

Visual RK - Event Poster Design

Event-Focused Visual Communication

Project Overview

This project highlights a set of event-focused visual assets created to carry campaign messaging across print and digital touchpoints. The work balances bold poster design, legible hierarchy, and consistent storytelling so each asset feels connected while serving a distinct communication need.

Poster Design System

The layout system was built to keep information clear, visually engaging, and adaptable across multiple deliverables. Typography, imagery, and spacing were shaped to help each poster stay expressive while still feeling part of a cohesive campaign family.

Key Design Principles

  • Bold and expressive poster design
  • Clear information hierarchy
  • Adaptable across multiple formats
  • Consistent storytelling
  • Cohesive campaign family
  • Print and digital optimization
